Beyond All Reason in the browser

Unofficial WebAssembly/WebGL2 port of the Recoil engine. Skirmish against one of BAR's built-in Lua AIs, or multiplayer with friends in their browsers. Needs a recent Chrome or Edge on a desktop GPU.
